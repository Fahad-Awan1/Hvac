What's in a Tune-Up

Our 21-point seasonal checklist

Spring: Cooling

  • Clean condenser & evaporator coils
  • Measure refrigerant pressures & superheat
  • Test capacitors, contactors & motors
  • Flush condensate drain line
  • Calibrate thermostat & check airflow

Fall: Heating

  • Inspect heat exchanger for cracks
  • Carbon-monoxide & gas-pressure tests
  • Clean burners & flame sensor
  • Test safety limits & ignition
  • Lubricate blower & check belts

Plan FAQ

Good to know

Still deciding? Ask Breeze, our assistant, for a quick recommendation.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with no fees. Yearly plans are refundable pro-rata minus any services already used.

Does a plan cover multiple systems?

Each plan covers one heating and one cooling system. Additional systems can be added at 50% off.

Is the plan transferable if I sell my home?

Yes, plans transfer to the new homeowner, which is a nice selling point.

Do you service systems you didn't install?

Absolutely. We maintain and repair all major brands, regardless of who installed them.
