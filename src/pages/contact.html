Before You Call

Quick answers

Common questions about booking and arrival.

How fast can a technician arrive?

Emergencies: usually within 60 minutes in our core area. Standard bookings: same or next day, with a 2-hour arrival window and a text when your tech is on the way.

Will I know the price upfront?

Yes. After diagnosis you'll get a flat-rate price to approve before any work starts. No hourly surprises.

Do I need to be home?

For indoor equipment, yes, or a trusted adult. For outdoor-only work we can often proceed with your permission and send photos.
