FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Can't find what you need? Ask our assistant (bottom right) or give us a call.

How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

Twice a year is ideal: your AC in spring and your furnace or heat pump in fall. Regular tune-ups prevent up to 85% of breakdowns and keep your warranty valid.

How long does a new AC or furnace installation take?

Most residential replacements are completed in a single day (4–8 hours). Full ductwork redesigns or new construction can take 2–3 days.

Do you offer emergency service on weekends and holidays?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered 24/7/365 by real people, and we aim to have a technician at your door within 60 minutes in our core service area.

Should I repair or replace my system?

A good rule: multiply the system's age by the repair cost. If it's over $5,000, or the unit is 15+ years old, replacement usually saves money long term. We'll always show you both options.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, with 0% APR options for up to 24 months and low monthly payments for up to 10 years, subject to approval. Try our payment calculator.
